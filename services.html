How projects typically run

1. Brief

What needs to happen, who it is for, and what “done” looks like. We agree scope, timeline, and budget before work starts.

2. Build or audit

Either a focused build, or a review of the current store, site, marketing and content with a clear list of changes.

3. Launch

Testing, handover, and the small operational details so your team can run things day to day.

4. Support

Optional retainer for updates, campaigns, content, and the small changes that pile up after launch.
