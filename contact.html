Frequently asked questions

How long does a typical project take?

A straightforward website or Shopify store is often a few weeks. More custom work or a larger catalogue takes longer. I will give a timeline once the scope is clear.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Retainers start from £250/month depending on how much operational help you need after launch.

Do you only work on Shopify?

Shopify is a core part of the web work, alongside websites, Klaviyo, and content. If you are on another stack, say so in the brief and we can see whether it is a fit.
